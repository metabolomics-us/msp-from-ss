How it works

Click 'Browse' and select a spreadsheet with one of these extensions: .xlsx, .xls, .csv, .ods, or .numbers, or a MS-DIAL "Alignment result" export (.txt). Then click 'Submit' to download a converted .msp text file. The downloaded file will have a .txt extension which you can rename to .msp.

Rows with no MS/MS spectrum are silently skipped (not left blank) for both spreadsheet and MS-DIAL AlignmentResult.txt uploads. For a MS-DIAL AlignmentResult.txt file, rows with an unidentified ("Unknown") metabolite name are kept as long as they have a spectrum.

Your spreadsheet must include columns with these labels: 'Average Rt(min)', 'Average Mz', 'Metabolite name', 'Adduct type', 'Formula', 'INCHIKEY', 'MS1 spectrum', and 'MSMS spectrum'.
